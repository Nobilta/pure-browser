Reader popup opener

This page exists so the reader typography bar can be checked on a real WebView: the article below is long enough to pass Readability's character threshold, and the image gives the page context sheet something to long-press.

Pure Browser renders an article in the live document, so the address bar still points at this page. The floating bar should change the size and the paper colour without reloading anything.

Reading on a phone is mostly about two numbers: how large the text is and how much space sits between letters. A third choice, the paper colour, decides whether the page is white, warm, or near-black, and it is independent of the app theme so a dark room does not force a dark article for someone who prefers paper.

The fixture keeps every paragraph at a reasonable length so the controls can be judged against real text rather than a single line. Lines should wrap evenly and the article should stay centred inside the page.

Typography controls are written through to a preference file. Leaving the reader and entering it again, or restarting the app, should preserve the last choice. The same values are handed to the controller before Readability runs, so the first paint already has the requested paper instead of flashing the default and then changing.

When the article is long enough, scrolling should remain native: the bar floats above the page and does not intercept touches outside its own bounds.
